Coventry, United Kingdom

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Warwick, supervised by Prof. Victor Sanchez.

My research focuses on semi-supervised learning and fine-grained visual recognition. I am interested in how local visual features, semantic information, and reliable pseudo-labels can help models learn from limited labelled data.

Research interests

01 / LEARNING

Learning with limited labels

Semi-supervised learning, pseudo-label reliability, and learning with imbalanced data.

02 / RECOGNITION

Fine-grained understanding

Discriminative object parts, local–global representations, and subtle visual differences.

03 / REPRESENTATION

Visual & semantic modelling

Vision transformers, state-space models, and visual–language semantic guidance.
